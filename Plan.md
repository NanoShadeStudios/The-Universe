The Universe

You start by clicking to gain cosmic dust , then you can buy from 3 repeatable upgrade: You(gives 1 cosmic dust per second and one more comsic dust per click), Multiplier(gives a multiplicative boost to cosmic dust), Temperature( gives a exponential boos to cosmic dust)
In this formula M(Y)to the power of T

you gain entropy based on total mass this prestige, entropy can be used to unlock single upgrades and tons more later on.


Clump
Effect: Boosts Cosmic Dust per click based on total You upgrades owned
Friction
Effect: Increases your Temperature ($T$) effectiveness based on total clicks made in the current reset 
Magnet
Effect: Multiplies You ($Y$) base output based on total Entropy accumulated
Swirl
Effect: Boosts Multiplier ($M$) based on the total combined levels of all three repeatable upgrades ($Y + M + T$).
Chain reaction
Effect: Multiplies all Cosmic Dust gain based on how many Single Upgrades you have purchased so far (e.g., ×1.5 per single upgrade owned).
